# Asset provenance

These initial graphics were created specifically for the autism-gaming prototype
using a small deterministic Python/Pillow drawing script. They are not copied
from a third-party asset pack.

Files:
- grass.png — tileable grass texture.
- player.png — 4-direction, 4-frame player sprite sheet.
- items.png — pixel icons for sword, pickaxe, axe, bow and arrows.
- shadow.png — soft pixel-style player shadow.

Separate environment and projectile images were added as original SVG assets:
- objects/trees/tree.svg — tree.
- objects/rocks/rock.svg — rock.
- objects/stumps/stump.svg — stump.
- projectiles/arrows/arrow.svg — arrow projectile.

No third-party artwork or external image-generation service was used for these
files. Keep future imported packs in separate folders and record their original
URL, author and exact license before committing them.
